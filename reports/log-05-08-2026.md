### What is Clever Hans behavior / phenomena?

The name Clever Hans is based off of the story that a horse named Hans was supposedly able to perform simple arithmetic; however, in reality, Hans relied on human body language cues to correctly guess the answer. In general, Clever Hans phenomena are associated with the perception that something like a living being or computer understands how to approach or solve a problem, when in reality, it relies on some spurious correlation or incorrect strategy. 

In machine learning, the most famous example of Clever Hans behavior is an image classifier identifying images of a horse based on the presence of a watermark / tag at the bottom corner of the image rather than the horse itself. In reinforcement learning, Clever Hans behavior can be seen as abusing the rules or mechanics of solving a problem, such that the problem is approached and solved in an unintended or invalid manner [Lapuschkin et al., 2019].
