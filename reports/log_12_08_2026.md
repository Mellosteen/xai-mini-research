## Relevant Knowledge

### Mean Absolute Error (MAE)

MAE is calculated by calculating the absolute difference between a predicted outcome versus the true outcome. These differences are then averaged by summing up all distances and dividing by the total number of data points. 

$$\frac{1}{n}\sum^n_{i=1}|y_i-\hat{y}_i|$$

$y_i$: the actual value
$\hat{y}_i$: the predicted value.

### Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)

RMSE is $$\mathrm{RMSE}=\sqrt{\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(y_i - \hat{y}_i\right)^2}.$$

### R-Squared (R2)

$$R^2=1-\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(y_i-\hat{y}_i\right)^2}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(y_i-\bar{y}\right)^2}$$

$\overline y$: the mean of true values.

### Linear Regression

For $$\hat y_i = ax+b,$$
$$a = \frac{\overline c_{xy}}{\overline\sigma_x^2},\\ b = \overline \mu_y - a\overline\mu_x,$$

where $\overline c_{xy}$: the empirical covariance between inputs and output
$\overline\sigma^2$: the empirical variance
$\overline\mu:$ the empirical mean

Do note that this is a simple formalation of the linear regression. The implementation will utilize methods from scikit-learn.
